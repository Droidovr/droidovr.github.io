Tilt — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Tilt, a mobile game developed by Droidovr ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, write to droidovr@gmail.com.

Information the game keeps on your device

Tilt has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Your progress, settings (volume, language), hint count and game statistics are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

Advertising

Tilt is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ads shown and your interaction with them;
  • diagnostic data about the performance of the ads.

This information is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with a Google consent form the first time it starts. Depending on your choice, you see personalized or non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy settings.

Personalized ads on Android

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

Tilt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. As the game itself does not collect personal data, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through the links above; you can also contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using other services (for example, analytics or in-app purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released. The effective date at the top shows the latest change.
